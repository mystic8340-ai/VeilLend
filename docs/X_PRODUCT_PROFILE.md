### Tweet 2 (The Solution):
How does VeilLend work?
1️⃣ An accredited issuer attests to your financial attributes (income, credit score, repaid loans).
2️⃣ You generate a ZK proof of your credit tier locally in your browser.
3️⃣ The Midnight smart contract verifies the proof and disburses funds.
Zero personal data touches the chain.
